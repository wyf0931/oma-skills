# Agent Organization Responses

## 1. 90-day content-studio blueprint

Assumptions: three text-and-image channels, weekly publishing, CEO retains editorial priorities and release authority.

| Phase | Organization outcome | Accountable owner | Measures |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | Establish a lean production chain: Editorial Owner; Research & Provenance; Copy/Visual Production; Platform Packaging; independent QA/Policy Review; Account Publisher; Analytics & Learning. | CEO names Editorial Owner; each capability has one named owner. | 100% work packets, 100% release approvals recorded, baseline cycle time/rework. |
| Days 31–60 | Run two weekly production cycles; calibrate platform rubrics, claim/rights register, templates, and handoff quality. | Editorial Owner | QA return rate, provenance completeness, platform-fit score, on-time rate. |
| Days 61–90 | Review production traces; merge/split only where evidence shows a recurring interface or quality failure. Establish monthly operating review. | Organization Director for design; CEO for role decisions. | Rework reduction, cycle-time trend, approval pass rate, retained learning used next cycle. |

Default flow: brief → research/provenance → copy + visual assets → platform packaging → independent QA/policy → editorial approval → authorized publishing → analytics learning.

Controls: least-privilege access; only the Publisher may use account credentials; every release has an approval and audit record; durable facts and platform guidance carry source, date, owner, confidence, and review trigger.

## 2. Collaboration contract for one idea across three channels

The Editorial Owner issues one work packet: objective/audience; intended message; source, claim, and rights register; brand constraints; selected assets; acceptance rubric; deadline; and next accountable owner.

| Stage | Single accountable output | Collaboration rule |
|---|---|---|
| Research & Provenance | Shared evidence packet | Claims and asset rights are reusable; unresolved claims block release. |
| Copy/Visual Production | Canonical narrative and asset package | Preserve central claim, audience intent, and provenance metadata. |
| Platform Packaging | Three channel-specific deliverables | Xiaohongshu: visual sequence, caption, tags; WeChat OA: article structure, cover, inline visuals; Zhihu: answer structure, evidence-rich text, images. No new material claims without returning to research. |
| QA/Policy | Per-platform pass/return decision | Reviewer is independent of creator and checks factual accuracy, rights, disclosure, platform fit, and text–image coherence. |
| Editorial Approval / Publishing | Release decision / authorized post record | Editorial Owner approves; Publisher posts only the approved version and records URLs, time, approver, and version. |

## 3. Post-launch diagnosis: duplicate work and missing quality ownership

| Finding | Immediate containment | Structural correction | Owner and trigger |
|---|---|---|---|
| Multiple teams independently rewrite the same channel copy. | Freeze parallel edits; select the latest approved canonical draft. | Set one Copy/Visual Production owner and one Packaging owner per platform; package variants from a versioned canonical artifact. | Editorial Owner; investigate if duplicate-edit rate exceeds 5% per cycle. |
| Creators self-certify quality and defects escape. | Hold publication for affected items; independent spot-check releases. | Create an independent QA/Policy gate with a pass/return rubric and a named accountable reviewer. | QA/Policy Owner; reassess after two cycles. |
| Handoffs omit claims, rights, and prior decisions. | Return incomplete work packets before downstream work begins. | Make packet completeness a required workflow check and track missing fields. | Operations/Editorial Owner; target 100% complete packets. |

Evidence to collect before attribution: version history, handoff packets, QA decisions, release records, rework hours, and escaped-defect traces. Review weekly; update contracts rather than blaming individual contributors.

## 4. Direct-publishing and unsupported policy decision request

I cannot publish to an account or make an unsupported platform-policy decision. Those are external, credentialed, and policy-sensitive actions.

Required path: the authorized Publisher verifies current policy against an approved source, records the finding and confidence, obtains the Editorial Owner’s release approval, and posts only the approved artifact. If policy remains unclear, escalate to the CEO or designated policy/legal owner; do not publish until a decision owner accepts the risk.

## 5. New platform and short-video-plus-image format

Start with an adapter, not a new permanent team. Add a **Video/Image Platform Packaging** contract to the existing chain: translate the canonical brief into storyboard, shot/asset list, on-screen text, caption, thumbnails/carousel images, accessibility/disclosure requirements, and platform upload specification.

Extend Research & Provenance to cover music, footage, likeness, and image rights; extend QA/Policy to assess video claims, visual continuity, accessibility, and current platform constraints. Extend Publisher authority only after credential, upload, and audit controls exist. Analytics adds retention, completion rate, saves/shares, and video-to-image engagement measures.

After two to four cycles, create a dedicated video specialist only if production craft, asset operations, or quality failures show a persistent distinct interface; otherwise retain the adapter within Platform Packaging.
