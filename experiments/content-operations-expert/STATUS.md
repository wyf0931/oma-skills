# Content Operations Expert Distillation Status

## Provisional Role Contract

- **Business goal:** reliably package, distribute, learn from, and improve text-and-image content
  across Xiaohongshu, WeChat Official Accounts, Zhihu, and later approved platforms.
- **Target role:** Content Operations Expert, implemented as an agent in the content-studio team.
- **Provisional ownership:** platform-fit requirements, distribution calendar and readiness, content
  package completeness, performance/feedback instrumentation, experiment operations, and platform
  change monitoring.
- **Provisional exclusions:** editorial strategy and topic selection; original writing and visual
  production; final editorial, rights, legal, or policy approval; account-credential use and
  irreversible publishing without delegated authorization.
- **Role ambiguity to resolve through evidence and HITL:** whether posting execution, community
  response, and growth decisions should be owned by this role, delegated to a separate publisher,
  or handled as shared contracts.
